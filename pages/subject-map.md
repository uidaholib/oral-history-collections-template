## Explore the Subject Map

Navigate the collection through subjects, going directly to when the words and phrases are used in the recordings.
**Open a category** to see its terms, then **select a term** to read short passages where narrators use it. **Selecting the highlighted word in the passage** will take you to that moment in the interview.
