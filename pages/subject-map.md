## Explore the Subject Map

Narrators often define the same idea in very different ways.
Open a category to see its terms, then choose a term to hear five passages where narrators use it, drawn at random from across the collection.
Select **Show five more** to compare another set of voices.

Each highlighted term links to the exact moment in the interview, and the recording starts playing from there.
