# How to Use This Website
{: .howto-title}

More than 300 interviews, recorded in the 1970s, with people who remembered life in Latah County and nearby places. Every interview has the audio recording and a full written transcript, and all of it is searchable. You do not need to know who anyone is, or what to look for, to start.
{: .lead}

## Two ways in

Pick the one that fits what you are after. You can switch at any time.

## Discover Interviews

Use the **Discover Interviews** menu at the top of the page.

### Browse All

[Browse All]({{ '/browse.html' | relative_url }}) shows every interview as a card.

- **Search by keyword.** Try a name, a place, or a word like *sawmill*. Partial words work, and quotation marks find an exact phrase.
- **Narrow with filters.** Limit the list by subject, occupation, where someone lived, who did the interview, and more. Pick several to combine them.
- **Advanced Search** looks in one particular field, such as a date range.

### Word clouds: subject and location

Bigger words appear in more interviews. Click a word to see the interviews that match.

- [Browse by Subject]({{ '/subjects.html' | relative_url }}): topics and occupations, such as logging, farming or teaching.
- [Browse by Location]({{ '/locations.html' | relative_url }}): places people talk about and places they lived.

Good for wandering when you do not have a question yet.

### Timeline

The [Timeline]({{ '/timeline.html' | relative_url }}) lays the interviews out by date, so you can see when the recordings were made.

## Explore Transcripts

Browsing finds interviews. The **Explore Transcripts** menu finds *passages*. The words in the transcripts have been tagged by subject, so you can follow a topic through every interview that mentions it, even if it is only a few sentences in an hour of talk.

### Subject Map

The [Subject Map]({{ '/subject-map.html' | relative_url }}) is a branching diagram of subjects.

1. Open a category on the left, such as *Labor*, *Culture* or *Environment*.
2. Keep opening branches until you reach a term, such as *threshing*, *moonshine* or *Christmas*.
3. Select the term to read short quotations from the interviews where it comes up.
4. Select the highlighted word in a quotation to go to that moment, with the audio ready to play.

### Subject List

The [Subject List]({{ '/subject-list.html' | relative_url }}) has the same terms as one A to Z list, like the index at the back of a book. Some terms have a short definition, which helps with logging jobs and old-fashioned words. Filter the list with the box at the top, or jump ahead by letter. Selecting a term opens the same quotations as the map.

{: .howto-note}
**Map or list?** Choose the map if you like to browse by theme, and the list if you already have a word in mind.

## Inside an interview

Each interview has its own page, with the recording and transcript together. Several tools help you find your way around a long conversation.

- **Time stamps.** Each transcript line has one. Select it and the recording jumps to that spot.
- **Description.** A short summary, the date and place, and the subjects covered. A quick way to decide if it is the one you want. **View Item Information** shows the full record.
- **Topics strip.** Colored blocks standing for the whole interview, start to finish. Each color is a subject. Hover to read what is said there, and select to jump to it.
- **Filter by Topic.** Hides everything except the lines about one subject, for example only the parts about food. **Reset Filters** brings it all back.
- **Transcript search.** Finds a word or phrase in this one interview and lets you jump to each match.
- **Index tab.** Many interviews have one next to the Transcript tab: the main points of the conversation, each with a time and a short summary. Often the quickest way into a long interview.
- **Narrator card.** A small card with their picture, birth year, occupation and home. Select the name to go to their page.
- **PDF.** Download or print the transcript.

## Narrator pages

Each narrator has a page that gathers everything about them in one place. Reach it from the narrator card on any interview page. It works as an introduction to the person before, or after, you listen.

- **Who they were.** Portrait, birth year, occupation, where they came from and where they lived.
- **All their interviews.** A card for each, with date, length, interviewer and main subjects.
- **Their places.** A map of the places mentioned in their interviews.
- **Their topics.** A word cloud across all their interviews. Select a topic to see other interviews on that subject.
- **A way into the rest.** Their occupation, interviewers and places are links to everyone else who shares them.

{: .howto-note}
**Follow a thread.** Start with someone interesting, see where they lived and what they did, then use the links to find others with similar lives.
